## The note

**Subject:** A few things — Europe door-to-door, December plans, HubSpot

Otto, Stacy,

A handful of things, some new and some tidying up. Headers so you can find your own.

---

**1. We can now offer door-to-door in Europe**

We've partnered with **Rowland Brothers**, who are one of the most established repatriation
firms in the UK. What this means for you:

- We can arrange **pickup and onward transport from most major European airports** through
  to the doorstep — a residence, a funeral home, wherever it needs to go.
- **This includes cremated remains**, which is a gap we've never been able to fill properly.
- Their drivers deliver direct, and they have tracking in their vehicles, so we can give a
  customer confirmation of delivery and a time — not just "it landed."

The honest caveat: **we don't have pricing yet.** Rowland are putting a sheet together. So
please raise this in conversations as something we now do, but **don't quote a number** — if
someone wants a price, bring the lane to me and we'll get it turned around.

Where this matters most is the shipment that used to end at a European airport with the
family arranging the last leg themselves. That was always the weakest part of what we sold.

---

**2. Stacy — on Mexico, that one goes to Cheryl**

Cheryl has been working on something with a Mexican carrier specifically to make us more
competitive there, so she's the right person for it rather than me.

One thing that would genuinely help her: **tell her which routes you're running into.**
Not "Mexico" — the actual city pairs where you're losing on price or can't get a

**3. Regional meetings — there's a template attached**

Three parts: the plan, the meetings themselves, and a short readout.

**Please save a blank copy and leave it blank — that one's your master.** Every week, take a
fresh copy, rename it with the week and the region, and fill that one in. Don't keep
overwriting a single running document. The whole point is being able to put twelve weeks side
by side and see what changed, and that only works if each week is its own file.

It's deliberately the same format every time. One week is a document; twelve weeks in the
same format are a picture of a region.

The one habit worth keeping: **fill it in the same day.** Not Friday. The detail that makes a
note useful is gone by Friday.

---

**4. The December plan — what I'm actually looking for**

I don't think I was clear enough about this, so let me try again. **I'm after strategic
guidance, not an activity list.**

**What we're trying to do, and why — two objectives:**

- **New business.** Funeral homes that have never booked with us.
- **Strengthening what we already have.** Recapturing the one-and-done and dormant homes,
  and deepening the relationships with the homes already shipping with us.

The second one is newer and easier to skip, and it's the one your call lists exist for. A
home that used us once and stopped already knows who we are. That is a cheaper conversation
than a stranger, and we have never systematically had it.

**How we do it:** shows, cold calls, location visits, testimonials, and getting people set
up on the portal.

So what I want in your December plan is: **which of those you're leaning on, in which part
of your territory, and why that choice and not another one.** If you think one of those five
doesn't work in your patch, say so — that's more useful to me than a plan that covers
everything evenly.

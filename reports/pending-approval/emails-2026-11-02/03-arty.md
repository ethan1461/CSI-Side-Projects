## The note

**Subject:** Ahead of Thursday — mostly "what do we already have?"

Hi Arty,

Looking forward to sitting down. Most of what I want to cover is establishing what we
already have rather than asking you to produce anything, so I thought I'd send it ahead.

Some background on why. We're trying to make Cargo Sales a genuinely data-driven business,
and I keep running into questions about our own economics I can't answer. I can tell you
what we charge on 6,900 routes and almost nothing about what they cost us.

*[**Optional — see notes, and check with the CEO first:** There's a second reason. We're
starting to think seriously about the next few years — succession, and the possibility of
bringing in outside capital or making acquisitions. That would mean our financials get read
by people outside the company for the first time in 23 years. I'd much rather find out now
what shape they're in than during somebody else's due diligence.]*

**The four I most want answered, and they're all quick:**

1. **Are our financials prepared on a cash or an accrual basis?**
2. **What do we produce each year — compiled statements, reviewed, audited?** And what
   would it take to move up a level?
3. **How is carrier cost booked?** When we pay an airline for a shipment, does that land in
   cost of goods sold or somewhere else — and is it ever netted against revenue rather than
   shown on its own line? I ask because I've been calculating gross margin from our rate
   tables and I need to know whether my numbers reconcile to yours or whether I'm measuring
   something different entirely.
4. **How many legal entities are there and what sits in each?** Specifically, how do Cargo
   Sales and Southern Cross Trading relate on paper? They operate as one business, but I
   don't know how clearly that's documented — and some of our operating certifications sit
   on the Southern Cross side.

**Then, if there's time — things I'd like copies of:**

- **Monthly profit and loss, FY2024 through FY2026 to date.** Monthly rather than annual —
  our volume has a strong seasonal shape, January runs about 15% above average and June
  about 8% below, and annual figures hide it.
- **Balance sheets for the same period**, and the **accounts receivable aging** as it stands.
  We pay carriers and then collect from funeral homes, so I suspect we're financing a
  meaningful amount of float and I've never measured it. *(Our own customer list shows
  roughly $856K of open balances across 191 accounts, with one at $284K — I'd like to
  understand how that sits against what you see.)*
- **Tax returns for the last three years.**
- **The chart of accounts**, so I can see how things are categorised before I ask about any
  of them.

**And the question I most want your honest answer on.**

If a third party — a lender, a buyer, an investor — looked at our financials next year,
**what would they take issue with, and what would they want normalised?**

I'm not asking for figures on anything personal and I'm not asking you to do the work. I
want to know where the soft spots are in advance, because I'd rather hear it from you than
from someone across a table. If the honest answer is "quite a lot," that's genuinely more
useful to me than reassurance.

Happy to work however suits you. If some of this is a report you can run in five minutes
and some is a project, tell me which is which and I'll prioritise.

Thanks Arty.

Ethan

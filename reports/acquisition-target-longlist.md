CSI does not serve, or a gateway where CSI has agents but no pickup, buys something new.

**Ranked by strategic fit, not availability**, as instructed. Availability is a separate
column, and in this market the best-fitting targets are the least available.

## 4 — Corrections: four entries that are not acquisition targets

**These are cheap corrections that prevent expensive mistakes.**

### 4.1 `swacargo.com` is Southwest Airlines Cargo — a supplier, not a competitor

It appears in CSI's auction insights at **22.74%** impression share and has therefore been
sitting in a competitor set. It is **an airline's cargo division** — a carrier, which is
to say a **counterparty on the cost side of CSI's spread**, not a broker competing for
funeral-home accounts. It is not acquirable and should be removed from the competitor set
and reconsidered as a carrier relationship. **Handoff: `market-intel` (B-03) and
`pricing-strategist`.** The interesting question it raises is not acquisition — it is why a
carrier is bidding on the keywords CSI's customers search.

### 4.2 `altogetherfuneral…` is Altogether / Foundation Partners Group — a prospect

A cremation-focused **funeral-home consolidator**, named in `CLAUDE.md` §5 and
`context/brain/accounts-and-pipeline.md` as a Tier 1 enterprise group and a CSI prospect.
It is **customer-side, not competitor-side.** Acquiring it is implausible on size and would
put CSI in competition with its own customers. **Excluded. It belongs to `sales-lead` and
`gtm-strategist`, not here.** Note for marketing: a customer and a vendor bidding against
each other on the same keywords is worth someone's attention.

### 4.3 `mortrack.com` is Cairnstack Software — adjacent technology, another lane

A chain-of-custody and case-management platform for funeral homes, mortuaries and removal
services, listed under the corporate names Cairnsoft / Cairnstack Software LLC, with a
small apparent user base ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10008971/MorTrack/);
[Software Advice](https://softwareadvice.com/fleet-management-software/mortrack)). **Not a
shipping competitor.** Conceivably a small technology tuck-in, but:

- It would be bought for capability, not volume, which is a different thesis.
- CSI already has an unresolved platform-vendor question (`capital-routes.md` Part 0.2).
  **Buying a second software asset before resolving the first is the wrong order.**
- `product` and `gtm-strategist` own the platform and partnership view. **Handoff, not a
  pipeline entry.** MorTrac was already flagged as a potential software partner at TFDA 2026
  (`accounts-and-pipeline.md`), which is probably the right frame.

### 4.4 The assistance and insurance channel is a channel, not a target list

**This reclassifies one of Ethan's three adopted target lanes** (`initiatives/acquisitions.md`:
"assistance/insurance-channel repatriation firms", Aug 2026) and the reclassification is the
useful part.

The firms that actually hold this channel, from public benefit documentation, are
**Assist America**, **AXA Assistance USA**, **International Medical Group (IMG)**,
**On Call International**, **RedpointWTP** and **Arch RoamRight**

**None is an acquisition target for CSI.** They are insurer-owned or operate at a scale

**So the lane should be a partnership and channel-development priority, not an acquisition

# TASK-0014 — Establish the brand awareness baseline CSI has never measured

**Owner:** marketing-lead
**Created:** 2026-11-02 by pm-orchestrator
**Due:** 2026-11-06
**Priority:** HIGH
**Status:** UNCLAIMED

## Objective
Ethan's mandate for this function is unambiguous: CSI has a brand awareness problem among
funeral directors and that has to change — "better known is better than best." He means it.

The problem is that **awareness has never been measured**, so there is no number to move, no
way to tell a good quarter from a lucky one, and no KR this objective can carry. Before
anything is spent on becoming better known, establish where we start.

This is deliberately **not** a campaign task. `§9.2` forbids new campaigns until the
measurement that reads them exists, and that question is TASK-0001's. This task is the
measurement for the brand objective itself, which has no such blocker and should have been
built a year ago.

## Line to the metric
North star — category leadership in repatriation, specifically whether CSI is the company
funeral directors call first. Awareness is the leading indicator of that and the only one
currently at zero visibility.

## Deliverable
`reports/brand-awareness-baseline.md`:

1. **What can be measured today, at no cost**, with the actual numbers pulled: branded search
   volume and its trend, direct traffic, share of voice against Eagle's Wings Air, Inman
   Shipping Worldwide and International Logistics Services in organic and AI search, AI
   citation rate on the questions a funeral director would actually ask, review and directory
   presence, LinkedIn follower and engagement base, and association-directory listings.
   **AI citation rate and quote requests from identified funeral homes are CSI's stated
   success metrics — not raw sessions** (`context/brain/learnings-and-principles.md`).
2. **The gap that free data cannot close.** Unaided and aided awareness among funeral
   directors is a survey question, not an analytics question. Specify the cheapest credible
   way to get it: sample size, the five questions, the distribution route (a state
   association, a show, an email to existing customers), and the cost. Ethan's instinct will
   be to spend; show him what $500 and a 150-response sample would tell us versus what a
   funded study would.
3. **The baseline table itself** — one row per metric, today's value, source, and how often it
   re-reads. This is the artifact that lets O3 and the brand objective carry a KR next quarter.
4. **The one thing you would change first** if the goal is being better known, with a cost and
   a measurable success threshold.

## Success criteria
- [ ] Every number is pulled live and dated, not estimated. A metric that cannot be pulled is
      listed as unavailable with the reason
- [ ] Competitor comparisons rest on observable public data only. **No Inman or ILS pricing
      figure** — CSI does not have it

      version that would still be credible
- [ ] The baseline table is re-runnable by someone else next quarter

## Constraints
**`§9.2` — no campaign, no channel, no spend.** This task measures; it does not promote. If
you conclude a campaign is the answer, that is a recommendation gated on TASK-0001 returning a
measurable conversion path.
**`§7.1` — publishing or scheduling any social post, ad, blog or website change needs Ethan's
written approval, every time.** So does any pricing claim, savings figure or competitive
comparison shown externally.
**`§7.4` governs every line you write, including internal examples.** "Loved ones," "families,"
"the person in your care." Never "cargo," "units," "product," "bodies," "inventory." No
urgency manipulation, no countdown timers, no "act now." No humor about death — none, not even
gentle. Read every line as if a grieving family might see it, because many will.
**Do not reuse existing collateral without checking it.** Brand kit v1 and the August Google
Ads copy were built on stale five-gateway and inbound-customs claims
(`context/brain/deliverables-index.md` → "Stale material in circulation",
`initiatives/google-ads.md`). Eight gateways is canonical; physical handling is export-only.
Any claim you intend to use must be cleared by TASK-0012's register first.
No year count is published until Ethan rules on C10.
Do not edit `context/brain/` directly; use `/brain-propose` (`§12`).

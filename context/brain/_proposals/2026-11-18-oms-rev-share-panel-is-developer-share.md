# The OMS "Total Rev Share Pool" panel is CSI's payment to its Developer, not a customer revenue share

## Current text (if any)

none (the panel was read in the live session as a customer-facing revenue-share mechanism)

## Proposed text

> The OMS Portal Revenue Tracking page carries a "Total Rev Share Pool" with five marginal tiers (1.5% at $0-$500K to 3.5% above $2M). These are the FreightFlow agreement's Ex. C §5 tiers: the calculation of what CSI owes its software Developer on customer-initiated portal revenue. It is not a revenue share offered to customers, no customer rebate exists, and the tiers are a confidential term of CSI's vendor agreement that must not be shown to SCI or any customer. Each portal-initiated dollar costs CSI 1.5% to 3.5%.

## Evidence

`reports/sci-portal-pilot-evidence.md` §3; `reports/freightflow-agreement-review.md §2.2`. Match is on the tier schedule and on the arithmetic ($500,000 - $12,458.44 = $487,541.56; 1.5% x $12,458.44 = $186.88); the page itself has not been seen by the agent team. Needs a one-line confirmation from Kyri or Alex.

## Why another agent would act differently

pricing-strategist, gtm-strategist and finance would otherwise model a customer revenue share that does not exist, and finance must treat portal adoption as a channel cost.

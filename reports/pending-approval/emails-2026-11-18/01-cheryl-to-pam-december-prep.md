## The note

**Subject:** Ahead of the 2nd — anything you'd like us to cover?

Hi Pam,

Hope all is well with you.

Ethan is meeting your vendor leadership on **2 December**, and I wanted to get ahead of it with you
rather than have it arrive cold.

**Two things, both easy to say no to.**

**First, is there anything you would like us to cover?** You know better than we do what matters to
that group and what they are being asked about at the moment. If there is something you would
rather we addressed — or avoided — tell us and we will build around it.

**Second, we would like to hear from a few of your directors.** Not a marketing exercise. We want
to put a few of their words in front of your team — what has actually worked when they have shipped
with us, and where we have been less good. It is more useful to your colleagues than anything we
could say about ourselves, and more useful to us than a polished quote.

**Would you help us pick who to ask?** A handful of people who have shipped with us often enough to
have a real view. If you would rather it came through you, or rather we left it, that is completely
fine and we will take the steer.

**One thing we are thinking of raising**, so it is not a surprise in the room. At the moment there
is no regular record going to anyone at SCI of how we have performed across the Dignity locations we
serve — volume, turnaround, where things went wrong. We would like to start sending one monthly, to
whoever the right person is. We think it would be more useful to you than to us, and we would rather
you had the thought in advance than hear it for the first time on the 2nd.

**On timing** — I know the holiday week swallows most of what is left. If it is easier to talk for
ten minutes than to write back, Ethan or I can call whenever suits you this week or early next.

Thanks Pam, and we are looking forward to it.

Warm regards,

Cheryl

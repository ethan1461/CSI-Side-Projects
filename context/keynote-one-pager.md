KEYNOTE SPEAKER PROPOSAL

# Cheryl Silvey

CEO, Cargo Sales International  |  A Quarter Century of Bringing Loved Ones Home

For more than 25 years, Cheryl Silvey has been the person funeral directors call when the impossible needs to happen. From sitting U.S. Congressmen and foreign heads of state to celebrities and the hundreds of thousands of everyday families she has served, Cheryl has helped move loved ones to nearly every country on Earth — through wars, through pandemics, and through every operational challenge the global air-cargo system can produce.

As CEO of Cargo Sales International and one of the longest-serving female CEOs in the funeral care industry, Cheryl brings funeral directors a perspective no one else in the room can offer.

## A unique voice for your members

- 25+ years dedicated exclusively to serving the funeral profession

- One of the longest-tenured female CEOs in the funeral care industry

- Personally involved in shipments to nearly every country in the world

- Trusted with the highest-stakes cases — former U.S. Congressmen, foreign dignitaries, and high-profile public figures

- Led global remains transport through armed conflicts, the COVID-19 pandemic, natural disasters, and consulate and customs crises

## About Cargo Sales International

Headquartered in Miami with dedicated agents in MIA, JFK, IAD, IAH, and LAX, CSI has spent 23 years moving more than 12,000 loved ones every year through the six largest gateways in the United States. CSI operates 24/7/365 with no booking fees and no call centers — every funeral home reaches a knowledgeable, dedicated member of the CSI team, every time.

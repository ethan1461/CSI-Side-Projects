Not "would you give us a testimonial." That invites a no and sounds like marketing.

> *"We are sitting down with your corporate team in a couple of weeks to talk about how we are
> doing across Dignity locations. If there has been a case where we got something right for you,
> would you be comfortable if we mentioned it — or said it in your words?"*

**Two or three sentences is plenty.** What makes one land with a vendor leader: a specific case, a
specific difficulty, and what CSI did. Not adjectives.

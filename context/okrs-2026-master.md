# Directors of Bookings – Kim Soto & Susej Roffe

## Role Overview

The Directors of Bookings oversee booking operations, team performance, and OMS adoption.

## Key Responsibilities

- Manage bookings team

- Ensure accurate booking execution

- Lead OMS adoption

- Handle escalations

- Improve operational workflows

## Success in This Role Looks Like

- High booking accuracy

- Well‑trained bookings team

- Reduced errors and escalations

- Operational readiness for growth

## Reporting & Collaboration

Report to the CEO and manage booking agents.

## Performance Scorecard

### Objective

Deliver scalable and accurate booking execution.

### Key Results (Bonus‑Eligible)

#### KR1: Maintain booking accuracy ≥ 95% -- $1,750/each bonus eligible

Accuracy is measured by bookings completed without internal errors such as incorrect airline, routing mistakes, documentation issues, or airline rejections caused by booking execution.

Booking Accuracy: A booking is considered “accurate” if it is completed without any of the following errors attributable to the bookings team:

Errors That Count Against Accuracy:

- Incorrect airline selected

- Incorrect flight number or flight date entered

- AWB mismatch (first 3 digits incorrect for airline)

- Incorrect routing (arrival before departure, wrong destination airport, etc.)

- Missing required documentation (death certificate, BTP, consular docs)

- Incorrect consignee/shipper information

- Airline rejection due to booking error

- Failure to include required packing instructions

- Incorrect trucking or pickup details

- Incorrect international document flagging

- Missing OMS-required fields

- Any booking requiring correction after airline submission due to data entry error

Errors That Do NOT Count:

- Airline schedule changes

- Weather delays

- Airline operational failures

- Funeral home failing to provide required documents on time

- Last-minute customer-driven changes

How It Is Measured:

Accuracy % = (Total bookings – bookings with internal errors) ÷ total bookings

*Measured monthly and averaged quarterly.

#### KR2: Reduce booking turnaround time by 25% -- $1,500/each bonus eligible

#### This tracks efficiency improvements from booking initiation to airline submission or confirmation. Improvements should come from improved workflows, training, and OMS usage—not from cutting quality or skipping controls.

#### Turnaround Time: Measured from the time booking request email is received TO Time booking request is submitted to airline

#### Exclusions:

#### Time waiting for missing documents from funeral home

#### Airline system outages

#### After-hours waiting periods outside staffed coverage window

#### What Does NOT Qualify:

#### Cutting quality checks

#### Skipping documentation validation

#### Submitting incomplete bookings

#### KR3: Achieve 100% OMS adoption

#### All bookings must be created, managed, and completed within the OMS, with no parallel shadow systems such as spreadsheets, emails, or external notes. System usage is confirmed through logs and workflow audits.

#### KR4: Increase agent throughput by 15% -- $1,500/each bonus eligible

Measured as the average number of bookings completed per agent while maintaining accuracy standards.

Throughput: Average number of completed bookings per agent per month. Measured only after the OMS backend stabilization baseline is established (Q1 baseline).

Throughput does NOT count if accuracy drops below 95%!

# Booking Agents – Maya, Sue, Abe, Renee

## Role Overview

Booking agents execute domestic and international bookings accurately using the OMS.

## Key Responsibilities

- Process bookings in OMS

- Validate airline and customer data

- Coordinate with airlines and funeral homes

- Ensure documentation accuracy

- Meet turnaround time SLAs

## Success in This Role Looks Like

- High booking accuracy

- Consistent OMS usage

- Positive customer interactions

- Strong collaboration

## Reporting & Collaboration

Report to the Directors of Bookings.

## Performance Scorecard

### Objective

Execute accurate and timely bookings.

### Key Results (Bonus‑Eligible)

#### KR1: Meet booking volume targets

#### This measures productivity relative to expected workload and case complexity. Consistent achievement of volume expectations is required, while exceeding targets without quality degradation is rewarded.

#### KR2: Maintain ≥99% booking accuracy

#### This tracks bookings that require no correction, airline follow-up, or internal rework. Accuracy reflects attention to detail, professionalism, and adherence to process.

#### Accuracy is tracked by:

#### OMS error logs

#### Airline corrections

#### Internal audit review

#### KR3: Meet SLA turnaround ≥95%

#### This measures responsiveness and reliability from booking initiation to submission. Delays caused by avoidable errors or missed follow-ups negatively impact this metric.

#### Excludes:

#### Customer delays

#### Missing documentation

#### Airline outages

#### KR4: Demonstrate consistent OMS usage

All bookings must be created, edited, and finalized within the OMS. System logs confirm compliance, and reliance on manual workarounds or side channels negatively impacts performance.

#### KR5: New Business Development -- $750/each bonus eligible

For any net-new funeral home (has never booked with CSI before), that you have directly sourced the business of and generates $2000+ in revenue this year, you will receive an additional $750 towards your bonus. This is meant to incentivize you to try and get new business during hours where you are not needed to support on the booking’s workflow. You must have clear, written documentation detailing relationship origination or something to verify that you sourced the opportunity.

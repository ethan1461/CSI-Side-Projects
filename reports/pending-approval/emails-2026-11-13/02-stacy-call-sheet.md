## The note

**Subject:** Your call list — 109 accounts, ranked

Stacy,

Attached is your call list. Some background first, because it changes how to read it.

We finally got our full shipment history out of the old system — every booking since 2022. It told
us something we had wrong. We'd assumed flat volume meant we weren't winning enough new funeral
homes. **We're winning plenty. We lose them again.** Of the homes that shipped with us in 2023,
close to half have placed no booking at all this year.

So this isn't a prospecting list. **It's a list of people who already know us.**

**You have 109 accounts**, in three groups:

- **15 at risk** — still shipping, but less. **Call these first.** Around 358 bookings a year
  between them
- **25 dormant** — stopped altogether
- **69 one-and-done** — shipped once and never came back

Every row has that home's booking history and a reason to call. Your first three:

1. **Fuller Funeral Home & Cremation Services** (FL) — 58 bookings in 2024, 47 in 2025,
   **5 so far this year**
2. **Florida Funeral Home and Crematory** (FL) — 29 in 2024, 23 in 2025, **none in 2026**
3. **Guarino Funeral Home** (NY) — 27 in 2024, then nothing since September 2024

**On Phoebe at Palm — you were right to ask.**

Palm Mortuary in Las Vegas is an SCI location, and that relationship is managed at their corporate
level. Cheryl is reaching out to our senior contact there this week to ask how they'd like it
handled. **You'll have an answer within the week.**

Until then: **Palm Mortuary & Cemetery is call 116 on your sheet and it's marked HELD. Please don't
call it**, along with the other 24 held rows at the bottom — all the same situation, all waiting on
the same conversation. I'll tell you when they're clear.

Checking before you acted was exactly right, and it got you a better answer than going ahead would
have.

**Two other things:**

1. **If a partner account appears, bring it to me.** Bergen, National Mortuary Shipping,
   International Mortuary Shipping and Las Rosas are customers *and* partners. They should be
   filtered out, but stop if one slipped through.
2. **Phone numbers aren't on the sheet** — pull them from HubSpot or the OMS account page.

**On the calls.** These are directors who used us and stopped, and most won't remember deciding to.
The honest opening works best: we noticed, we wanted to check nothing went wrong. Not a win-back
pitch. If they've gone elsewhere, that's worth knowing and worth asking about plainly.

**The part I'd most like done properly** — four blank columns on the right: outcome, reason code,
one sentence of what they said, next action.

**We have never once asked a funeral home why it stopped using us.** Not in 23 years. Everything we
believe about why accounts leave is inference. Forty or fifty filled-in rows would be the first real
evidence we've ever had, and it will shape next year more than the calls themselves will.

Congratulations again on the first sale, by the way.

Shout if anything looks wrong — you'll spot things I can't.

Ethan

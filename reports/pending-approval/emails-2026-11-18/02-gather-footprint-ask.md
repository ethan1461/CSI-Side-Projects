## Notes for Ethan — delete before using

**What this is for.** It resolves the one thing that decides whether Gather is a co-marketing
relationship or a distribution channel: how much of their base already ships with CSI. Until
that is bracketed, any revenue-share percentage CSI names is a guess (`§7.3`).

**Why state counts and not a list.** Neither side has to open its customer book. State-level
counts are enough to bracket the overlap against CSI's footprint, and they are a normal thing
to exchange early in a partnership conversation. **No list changes hands in either direction.**

**On offering CSI's side of it.** The draft offers reciprocity, because an ask with nothing
behind it usually gets a vague answer. What CSI would be giving is state-level counts of
funeral homes shipped for — business-level aggregate, no customer names, nothing from an
operational export, so it is `§7.2`-clean. **It is still CSI's distribution footprint, which
is competitively useful to someone.** Your call whether to offer it up front or hold it until
they ask. If you would rather not, cut the second paragraph; the ask still works.

**Do not characterise CSI's volume more precisely than this.** "Around a thousand funeral
homes last year" is the right altitude. The exact figure is entangled with the partner-routing
correction and is not a number to put in front of a counterparty this week.

**`market-intel` is researching Gather publicly in parallel** and will report what can be
established without asking them anything — product, customer profile, geography, whether they
already have a shipping partner. **Read that before you send this**, in case it answers part
of the question for free.

# The portal is an additional channel; platform SLA does not bound service promises

## Current text (if any)

none

## Proposed text

> The portal is an additional booking channel, not the service. Customers can always book, manage and track by phone or email through the 24/7 named agent, so platform downtime degrades convenience and does not break CSI's service promise (Ethan, 2026-11-18). The platform's 24-hour Level 1 restoration and uptime terms matter only for a *written* SLA CSI might sign (TASK-0017). SCI proposition: free portal deployment across SCI locations in exchange for SCI promoting adoption; the $999 Elite tier is not offered.

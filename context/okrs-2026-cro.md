# Chief Revenue Officer (CRO) – Vadim Shapiro

## Role Overview

The CRO drives net‑new revenue through acquisition of new funeral home customers in priority regions.

## Key Responsibilities

- Execute strategic business development plan

- Acquire new funeral home customers

- Expand CSI presence in priority markets

- Attend trade shows and industry events

- Support transition of finance‑related responsibilities to CFO

## Success in This Role Looks Like

- Meaningful revenue from new customers

- Expanded presence in targeted regions

- Predictable growth without operational strain

- Progress toward finance responsibility transition

## Reporting & Collaboration

Reports to the CEO and works with CSO, CFO, and operations leadership.

## Performance Scorecard

### Objective

Expand CSI’s revenue footprint through new customer acquisition.

### Key Results (Bonus‑Eligible)

#### KR1: Generate Net‑New Revenue -- 3.0% bonus eligible

Revenue must come from funeral homes that have NEVER previously booked with CSI, must complete at least three shipments in 2026, and must be in approved priority regions. Accounts affiliated with SCI (outside of the premier list – ask Ethan/Cheryl for this), Northstar, Foundation Partners, or Impact Partners are excluded. Revenue must be documented and relationship origination verified.

This is the primary performance driver. Revenue must meet all the following criteria:

Funeral home has NEVER previously booked with CSI

Located in designated priority regions

Completes minimum of 5 shipments in 2026

Revenue is documented in accounting system

Relationship origination is documented

Example Compensation Structure: 3% of qualified net-new revenue generated

$250,000 → $7,500

$500,000 → $15,000

$1,000,000 → $30,000

#### KR2: Secure 50 net‑new funeral homes, with each new funeral home generating ≥ $2,000 in revenue -- $5,000 bonus eligible

Funeral homes must be new CSI customers and generate at least $2,000 in revenue in 2026 to count toward this target.

#### KR3: Transition 50% of billing responsibilities to CFO

A documented transition plan is created and responsibilities are gradually transferred to Finance while maintaining operational continuity.

At the end of 2026, performance will be evaluated to see if you have met the expectations of the role.

## Business Model & Value Proposition

### What CSI Does

CSI is an **end-to-end shipment orchestration partner** for human remains logistics. We act as the "control tower" between:

- **Origin side:** Funeral home + removal/embalming/casket prep + local permitting
- **Air side:** Airline cargo booking + acceptance + handling + flight movement
- **Destination side:** Airline cargo release + receiving funeral home/agent + local delivery
- **International layer:** Consulate/embassy compliance, apostilles, translations, legalization, destination-country rules

### Our Competitive Moat (The CSI Difference)

We win on three dimensions:

1. **Cost:** We are a COMPLETELY FREE service for funeral directors
   - Funeral directors pay the published airline rate (same as calling the airline direct)
   - We don't charge booking fees (unlike competitors who hide fees)
   - We transparently explain our carrier discount model when asked

2. **Convenience:** We are an all-in-one service
   - 5-6 routing options across multiple carriers (vs 1-2 from competitors or airlines)
   - 24/7/365 live agents (not a call center) with 30min-1hr turnaround
   - Full domestic and international support
   - International paperwork handling (translations, apostilles, consulate processing)
   - Pickup and delivery coordination

3. **Coverage:** Multi-carrier relationships with specialized rates
   - Partnerships with just about every major carrier
   - Ability to optimize routing based on SLA, cost, and reliability
   - Coverage for both whole body and cremated remains shipments

### What Makes This Hard (Why CSI Exists)

Human remains shipments combine:
- **Time-sensitive operations** (flight cutoffs, weekend closures, limited cargo acceptance windows)
- **Nonstandard rules** (each airline's acceptance requirements + facility-specific quirks)
- **Jurisdictional paperwork** (state/county forms, transit permits, embalming affidavits)
- **International regulatory variance** (country-by-country, consulate-by-consulate requirements)
- **High reputational stakes** (errors are family-facing failures, not just "delays")

CSI's model reduces three critical risks:
1. **Compliance risk** (docs rejected / consulate refusal / airline non-acceptance)
2. **Timing risk** (missed flights, cutoffs, handoff failures)
3. **Coordination risk** (multiple parties + unclear ownership)

### CSI's Operating Workflow

#### 1) Intake and case framing
- Collect decedent details, origin/destination funeral homes, timeline, routing constraints
- Produce clear plan: route options, timeline assumptions, critical path steps
- Create document checklist tailored to route + airline + destination-country rules

#### 2) Airline strategy + routing
- Select route optimizing for: earliest reliable uplift, cargo acceptance reliability, fewer handoffs, carrier rules
- Provide 5-6 routing options (not just 1-2)
- Use deep carrier + station knowledge to choose best pairing

#### 3) Booking creation + cargo facility coordination
- Create airline booking (AWB/cargo reservation)
- Confirm acceptance requirements and cutoffs with airline station
- Coordinate origin funeral home timing for delivery, security/acceptance, labeling
- Confirm destination receiving procedures

#### 4) Documentation management
- Validate and sequence domestic doc packages (death certificate, transit/burial permit, embalming certificate, attestations)
- Ensure airline acceptance on first attempt

#### 5) International paperwork & consulate processing (CSI's "moat" layer)
- Country-specific documents (permits, embassy letters, translations)
- Authentication/legalization steps (apostille or equivalent)
- Correct consulate selection based on jurisdiction
- FedEx/original document logistics
- Translations aligned to consulate formatting expectations
- Rework loops if consulate requests edits

#### 6) Day-of-flight execution
- Confirm remains delivered to cargo by cutoff
- Monitor cargo acceptance
- Track flight movement
- Confirm destination station release readiness
- Coordinate receiving party pickup and last-mile delivery

#### 7) Exceptions & recovery
- Rebook, reroute, or switch gateways when disruptions happen
- Communicate proactively with funeral director
- Preserve chain-of-custody clarity
- Make "next best plan" immediately actionable

**Critical Note:** CSI currently absorbs rebooking costs 99% of the time when carriers cancel flights. This creates significant margin erosion that needs to be addressed.

## Revenue Model & Economics

### Current Revenue Breakdown

**80% — Air Freight Arbitrage**
- We charge funeral homes the **published airline rate** (same as calling the airline directly)
- Airlines give us a **30-60% discount** depending on carrier and route
- Our revenue = the margin between published rate and discounted rate
- Funeral homes perceive this as "free" because they pay what they'd pay anyway, but get all our services bundled

**15% — International Paperwork Support**
- Explicit fees for translations, apostilles, consulate processing
- Currently cost-plus model via partnership with Bergen Funeral Services
- Pure services margin, high complexity = high value

**5% — Other Services**
- LHT (high-net-worth) arrangement plans
- Empty casket shipping (one-off basis)
- Special service requests

### Key Economic Realities

**Margin Drivers:**
- International routes typically yield higher margins than domestic (60% vs 30%)
- Route/carrier selection directly impacts profitability
- International complexity is where CSI's value proposition shines most

**Margin Challenges:**
- **Flight cancellation rework:** CSI absorbs rebooking costs 99% of the time
  - Rebooking labor (agent time to find new routing, coordinate, update docs)
  - Potential rate changes (new flight may have worse discount)
  - Opportunity cost (agent could've processed new booking instead)
  - "Quite a bit" of rework happens due to unpredictable flight cancellations
  - This is a major margin leak that needs quantification and mitigation

- **Cost-plus international paperwork:** Bergen partnership may limit margin capture
- **Thin carrier discounts on some routes:** 30% margin may not cover labor on complex cases
- **Uncompensated value delivery:** White-glove service, 24/7 availability, exception handling all "free"

### Transparency Philosophy

Unlike competitors who hide booking fees, CSI openly explains the carrier discount model when asked. This transparency is a trust differentiator and competitive advantage.

## CSI OMS Platform

### What We're Building

CSI OMS is an **operating system for human remains logistics**: it turns a messy, multi-party, exception-heavy workflow into a **repeatable, trackable, auditable process** that scales.

### Core Components

**A) Intake & booking creation**
- Multi-input intake (email parsing, attachments, manual entry)
- Drag/drop document capture
- Structured fields to eliminate back-and-forth
- "Case type" classification (domestic vs international; destination rulesets)

**B) Quote & routing engine**
- Facility + airline lookup
- Lane-based pricing and cost components
- Routing options with SLA expectations
- Rule-based constraints (cutoffs, weekend risk, embargo risk)

**C) Workflow engine + task management**
- Standard checklists by shipment type/country
- Task assignment, due dates, escalation triggers
- Internal "handoff contracts" (who owns what, when)

**D) Document management + compliance**
- Required doc checklist and validation status
- Version control (what changed, when, by whom)
- Country/consulate templates (translations, legalization steps)
- Audit trail for compliance and customer trust

**E) Shipment tracking + status visibility**
- Milestone tracking: booked → docs received → consulate submitted → accepted → uplifted → arrived → released → delivered
- Exception states and recovery workflows
- Customer-facing updates (event-driven)

**F) Roles & permissions**
- Role-based access (exec, bookings director, agent, partner, customer portal user)
- Controls for sensitive data and client separation

**G) Reporting & analytics**
- KPI dashboards: turnaround time by step, rework/rejection reasons, carrier performance by lane, agent productivity, revenue + margin by segment/lane/customer
- Later: QuickBooks integration + admin reporting tools

**H) Future: Airline API / automated tracking**
- Proactive alerts on flight changes, delays, acceptance updates
- Reduces manual checking and "surprise" failures

## Operating Assumptions & Principles

### Core Beliefs That Guide All Decisions

**1) Reliability beats price**
- Families and funeral directors optimize for certainty, not lowest cost
- CSI should price to protect reliability (and charge for urgency/complexity)

**2) Errors are existential (reputational)**
- A single failure can damage relationships across an entire community/network
- QA, checklists, and escalation clarity are not overhead—they're the product

**3) Handoffs create risk; control reduces risk**
- Every new party (driver, cargo station, receiving agent) is a potential failure point
- Last-mile strategy is growth strategy because it reduces failures AND adds revenue

**4) Complexity is pricing power**
- Consulates, special documentation, embargoes, tight timelines = premium value
- Always identify "complexity premiums" CSI can legitimately charge

**5) Tech must reduce work, not add it**
- OMS adoption will fail if it feels like extra admin
- Every feature should map to: fewer touches, fewer exceptions, faster customer updates, fewer errors

**6) Scale requires role clarity and operating cadence**
- Directors must own quality + throughput
- COO must own adoption + execution excellence
- CSO must own monetization + partnerships + segment strategy
- CEO/CFO must enforce financial discipline + strategic focus

**7) The business is a services engine with a product wedge**
- CSI must not lose services excellence while productizing
- Correct sequence: service excellence → systematize → productize → distribute

**8) Transparency is a competitive advantage**
- Unlike competitors who hide booking fees, CSI openly explains carrier discount model
- This builds trust and differentiates from sneaky competitors

**9) Growth is a flywheel**
1. Operational excellence (fast, accurate shipments)
2. → Trust (repeat business + referrals)
3. → Data + patterns (what fails, what causes delays, what lanes are profitable)
4. → Systematization (OMS automates best practice)
5. → Capacity + consistency (more bookings with same quality)
6. → Enterprise readiness (corporates require reporting and predictability)
7. → Bigger accounts + stickiness (portal and workflow lock-in)
8. → More data … repeats

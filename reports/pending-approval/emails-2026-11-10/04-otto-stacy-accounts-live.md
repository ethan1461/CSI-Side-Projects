## The note

**Subject:** Your accounts are live in HubSpot

Otto, Stacy,

Quick note — HubSpot looks different as of today, in a way that should make your week easier.

**You each own accounts now.** Until this week our CRM had contacts but no companies, which meant
there was no way to tell whether a name on a list had ever shipped with us. That's fixed. There are
now **2,306 funeral homes** in HubSpot as real company records, each carrying its history from our
booking system.

**Otto, 414 are yours. Stacy, 378 are yours.** Filter to "my companies" and you'll see them.

**What's on each record:**

- Whether they've ever booked with us, and how much — so you know immediately whether you're making
  a first call or a reconnection
- Lifecycle set from actual booking history, not guesswork. Roughly 2,276 are customers; the rest
  are genuine prospects
- Their ID from our booking system, so what you see ties back to what operations sees

**How we split them.** Otto has the Midwest, Stacy has the West. For the Northeast and South — where
a lot of the best names turned out to be — we split them between you by call priority, alternating
down the list. If a particular account obviously belongs with one of you for a reason the data can't
see, just say and we'll move it.

**Three things worth knowing before you dive in:**

1. **Some accounts are partners, not prospects.** Bergen (NJ and FL), National Mortuary Shipping,
   International Mortuary Shipping and Las Rosas are among our largest customers *and* among our
   closest partners. They're flagged and they're not in your lists. If one comes up, bring it to me
   rather than working it as an opportunity.
2. **Enterprise groups come through me.** Anything connected to SCI/Dignity, Carriage, Foundation
   Partners or Northstar — those relationships are managed centrally and a well-meaning call to a
   single location can cut across a conversation happening above it. Flag them to me and I'll tell
   you where it stands.
3. **Not every account is ready to call yet.** Some are homes that received a shipment from us
   rather than sent one, so they were never really our customer. We're running a short phone sample
   next week to work out which is which, and you'll both be part of that. Until then, work the ones
   marked as confirmed shippers first.

**One limitation to keep in mind.** The booking history on these records is a snapshot from our
system, not a live feed. It's accurate as of now, but it won't update itself — so if a number
matters in a conversation, check it rather than quoting the card.

I'd rather you tell me this is wrong or missing something than work around it quietly. If a list
looks off, or an account seems misfiled, say so — this is the first version and it'll get better
fastest if you push back on it.

Nice work this week, both of you.

Ethan

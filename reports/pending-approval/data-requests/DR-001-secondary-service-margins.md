# DR-001: revenue and margin on secondary services

## Why it matters

The rate spread is measured (~35% domestic, ~41% international,
`reports/carrier-margin-resolved.md`). The fee side — consulate support, pickup and
drop-off, and other charges — is not. Pricing, valuation, and the sales narrative all
need both halves. If we don't get it, the business gets modeled on rate spread alone,
which understates it by an unknown amount.

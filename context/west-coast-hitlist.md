# 1.  Your Territory at a Glance

You own the West Coast: California, Nevada, Arizona, Oregon, Washington, and Hawaii. Your home gateway is LAX — one of CSI’s five U.S. gateways (MIA, LAX, JFK, IAH, IAD) — and a real local advantage: CSI offers funeral-home pick-up service in the LAX market, which most national competitors don’t.

Two pools of business sit in your territory:

- Pool A — SCI / Dignity (warm). Roughly 376 SCI funeral-home rooftops in your six states. CSI is already vetted, approved, in SpenseMart, and on the preferred-vendors list — and SCI corporate is actively engaged with us on the new customer portal pilot. You are not a cold caller here.

- Pool B — Non-SCI (net-new). Everyone else: the other consolidator groups (Foundation Partners, NorthStar, Carriage) and the large field of independents. Any new business is good business — this is where you expand CSI’s footprint beyond the SCI relationship.

## 4c.  How to build the independent list (your highest-margin hunting ground)

Independents serving immigrant and faith communities are where CSI’s international specialty wins outright. To find them quickly:

- Pull member directories from the state associations — CFDA (California), and the funeral boards/associations in NV, AZ, OR, WA, HI.

- Search by community + metro: e.g., “Filipino funeral home Los Angeles,” “Korean mortuary LA,” “Vietnamese funeral Westminster,” “Armenian funeral Glendale,” “Chinese funeral San Francisco,” “Samoan/Tongan funeral” — these homes repatriate constantly.

- At every show and call, ask the Playbook referral question — independents refer other independents.

# 5.  Your First Two Weeks

A simple way to start fast without boiling the ocean:

- Days 1–3: Read the Playbook end to end. Memorize the value prop and the “Another tool in your toolkit” frame. Have your LAX pick-up advantage and the 5–6 routing options at your fingertips.

- Days 4–7: Work Tier 1 SoCal SCI homes — warm, approved, local. Lead with the Funeraria del Angel cluster and the Jewish/Chinese homes (international volume). Use Playbook Scenario 1.

- Days 4–7 (parallel): Build a list of 10 international-heavy LA independents using the 4c method. Begin Scenario 3 outreach.

- Week 2: Extend to San Diego, then plan a Phoenix and Las Vegas push. Coordinate with HQ on Palm Mortuary (Vegas) given the pilot. Log every name, manager, and callback time as you go.

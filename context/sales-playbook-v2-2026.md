# 6. Discovery Questions

ASK THESE ON EVERY CALL — LEARN BEFORE YOU PITCH DEEPER

## Volume & Mix

How many ship-outs do you do in a typical year?

Helps size the opportunity and tailor your pitch. High volume = emphasize speed and consistency. Lower volume = emphasize ease and 24/7 support.

Are those mostly domestic or do you handle international cases?

Opens the door to lead with CSI's international strength. Even 1 to 2 international cases a year is a meaningful opening.

What destinations are you shipping to most frequently?

You can offer a rough ballpark on the spot — an immediate way to demonstrate value.

## Relationships & Decision-Making

Who is the location manager or area manager here?

Always try to get a name. For SCI, this also surfaces the right contact for an area-level relationship as the campaign scales.

Have you ever had a shipment get delayed going through the airline directly?

Most experienced FDs have a story about interline departments. This opens a pain-point conversation CSI directly solves — 3+ day delays, highest tariff rates.

## International & Paperwork

When you have international cases, who handles the consulate paperwork and translations?

Your biggest opening. If they're managing it themselves or piecing it together — CSI's full-service international handling is an immediate, major value add.

# 7. Do's & Don'ts

PROFESSIONAL STANDARDS — KNOW THE LINE

## You Can

- Give rough quotes for a route — ballpark ranges are fine. Be clear it's an estimate pending confirmation from the team.

- Reference SCI corporate approval — CSI is vetted, approved, and in SpenseMart. This is factual and appropriate to emphasize.

- Position CSI as an additional option — you're adding a tool to their toolkit, not asking them to fire anyone.

- Point to the executive team and website — if a director wants to verify credentials or speak with someone senior, direct them to cargosalesinternational.com or offer to connect them with leadership.

## You Cannot

- Disparage competitors — never speak negatively about Eagle Wings Air, ILS, Inman, or anyone else. It damages trust immediately and reflects poorly on CSI.

- Make guarantees you can't keep — don't promise specific pricing without confirming with the team. Don't guarantee turnaround times outside standard ranges.

- Be pushy or disrespectful — this industry runs on trust and relationships. One bad interaction can close a door permanently. Walk away gracefully if someone isn't interested.

- Speak on behalf of SCI/Dignity corporate — you can say you're approved and on the preferred vendors list. Do not imply SCI is directing funeral homes to use CSI exclusively.

# 8. Frequently Asked Questions

QUESTIONS FUNERAL DIRECTORS COMMONLY ASK — YOUR ANSWERS

"How do you make money if it's free?"

We earn a margin from the carrier rates we've negotiated. You never pay a booking fee or service fee — the carrier compensates us for the volume we bring them. The price you see is what you pay, and it's almost always cheaper than going direct. Same model a travel agent uses.

"What's your turnaround time?"

30 minutes to 1 hour for most bookings. We have agents available 24/7/365 — you'll always reach a live person, never a voicemail or queue.

"What gateways do you work out of?"

We have agents at LAX, JFK, IAD (Washington D.C.), IAH (Houston), and our headquarters in MIA — with ORD (Chicago) in the works. Having agents at these major gateways is what makes us so nimble with routing and pricing options.

"Do you handle international shipments?"

Yes — and it's our specialty. We handle full customs clearance at MIA, LAX, and JFK for international inbound shipments, plus all consulate documentation, translations, apostilles, and any other international paperwork required.

"Do you do pick-ups?"

Yes — we offer pick-up services in MIA, IAH, and LAX, and we're expanding to ORD. If you're in one of those markets and need a pick-up, we have you covered.

"How do I book a shipment?"

Three ways: call us, email us, or go through our website at cargosalesinternational.com. You'll always reach a live person on the phone, any time of day or night.

"What airlines do you work with?"

We have specialized rates with virtually every major carrier — which is how we're able to give you 5 to 6 routing options per shipment. We also bypass airline interline departments entirely. That process takes 3+ days and comes at the highest tariff. We get you confirmed quickly and competitively.

"Are you new? I've never heard of you."

We've been in business for 25 years and work with tens of thousands of funeral directors worldwide, including Dignity Funeral Directors. SCI corporate has already vetted and approved us. We've grown primarily through referrals — we're just newer to proactively reaching out, which is exactly what this campaign is about.

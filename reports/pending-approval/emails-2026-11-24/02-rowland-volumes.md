## The three judgement calls in this draft

1. **The arrivals caveat leads, and is not buried.** Ethan confirmed CSI does not know the
   split between arrivals and confirmed ground jobs. The draft says this plainly before
   Rowland prices anything, and offers to start capturing it. Letting them quote a flat
   rate against an unstated assumption is the expensive version of this conversation.
2. **Ukraine is answered as zero, not as unavailable.** The distinction matters — it tells
   them not to build a conflict-zone process for CSI volume that does not exist.
3. **Middle East and South America are CSI's own definitions,** stated in full in both the
   email and the workbook, with an offer to recut. Neither is a standard region, and a
   silent definition is how two parties agree on a number and mean different things.

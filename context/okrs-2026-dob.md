# Directors of Bookings – Kim Soto & Susej Roffe

## Role Overview

The Directors of Bookings oversee booking operations, team performance, and OMS adoption.

## Key Responsibilities

- Manage bookings team

- Ensure accurate booking execution

- Lead OMS adoption

- Handle escalations

- Improve operational workflows

## Success in This Role Looks Like

- High booking accuracy

- Well‑trained bookings team

- Reduced errors and escalations

- Operational readiness for growth

## Reporting & Collaboration

Report to the CEO and manage booking agents.

## Performance Scorecard

### Objective

Ensure the bookings organization operates reliably, professionally, and at scale while transitioning fully onto the OMS platform.

### Key Results (Bonus‑Eligible)

#### KR1: Achieve 100% OMS Adoption Across the Bookings Team

All bookings must be created, managed, and completed within the CSI OMS system. The goal of this KR is to eliminate parallel processes, spreadsheets, or manual workarounds and ensure the OMS becomes the single source of truth for all bookings.

Success will be measured by:

- Every booking initiated and completed through the OMS platform

- No use of parallel tracking systems (spreadsheets, personal notes, email-only tracking, etc.)

- Booking agents consistently entering required booking data fields within OMS

- Directors actively monitoring system usage and correcting deviations

#### KR2: Maintain Customer Satisfaction 4.5/5

#### The bookings team is the primary interface between CSI and its customers. This KR ensures that as systems and workflows evolve, customer experience remains consistently high. Customer satisfaction will be measured through structured surveys sent twice per year to CSI’s top 20 customers.

The survey will measure areas such as:

- Every booking initiated and completed through the OMS platform

- No use of parallel tracking systems (spreadsheets, personal notes, email-only tracking, etc.)

- Booking agents consistently entering required booking data fields within OMS

- Directors actively monitoring system usage and correcting deviations

The average score across all survey responses must be 4.5 or higher out of 5. Directors are responsible for reviewing feedback, identifying patterns, and implementing operational improvements where necessary.

#### KR3: Reduce General Operational Error Rate in Booking Execution

This KR focuses on improving overall operational reliability and reducing mistakes that create friction for customers or require additional operational work.

Errors included in this metric may include:

- Incorrect bookings requiring rebooking

- Incorrect flight or routing information

- Miscommunication with customers regarding shipment details

- Missing or incorrect documentation that delays a shipment

- Booking mistakes requiring internal correction or airline follow-up

- Agent-led failures that result in shipment delays or rework

Errors not included in this metric:

- Airline delays or schedule changes

- Customer failure to provide required documents

- Weather or operational disruptions outside CSI’s control

Success will be evaluated based on:

- Reduction in overall booking-related errors over time

- Fewer escalations requiring director-level intervention

- Fewer cases requiring rebooking due to internal mistakes

Directors are responsible for implementing training, workflow improvements, and OMS usage practices that reduce these errors across the team.

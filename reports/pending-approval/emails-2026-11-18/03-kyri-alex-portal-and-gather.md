## The note

**Subject:** Portal tracking emails — yes, plus a few questions

Kyri, Alex,

Short answer on the tracking email: **yes, let's do it.** Sending the portal link right after
the confirmation goes out is the cheapest adoption lever we have, and it catches the customer
at the one moment they actually want the information rather than us pushing it at them. The
30-minute refresh where there's an API and the carrier tracking page where there isn't is the
right call — partial coverage beats waiting for full coverage.

Good instinct on excluding the partner-routed customers. That one matters more than it looks:
a tracking email landing at a home that reaches us through NMS tells that home it's a CSI
customer, and those relationships are ones we're trying to deepen, not confuse.

**One question on the portal, and it's just a question.** Do you know what the revenue
tracking page actually filters on — whether a booking counts as portal revenue based on where
it originated, or on any portal activity against it? I'm not chasing anything, I'd just
rather understand how the number is built before we start driving traffic at it.

**Then two on Gather, while it's fresh.**

When they demoed, did you see anything in their case screen that captures **where a body is
going** — a destination, disposition, out-of-state or shipping field, anything like that? I'm
asking because it's the thing that decides whether this partnership is worth real money to
us. If Gather can tell us what share of their customers' cases involve a transfer out of
state or abroad, we'll know in a day whether we're talking about fifty homes with genuine
repatriation volume or five hundred who almost never need us. If they can't, we're guessing.

And did an **API** come up? Everything public suggests they don't have one, but that's thin
and I'd rather hear what they told you.

**Last one.** Where did the 800 come from — did they say it, and did they mean funeral homes
as firms or as locations? Their own site says "500+ funeral homes" in one place and "2,000+
deathcare professionals" in another, so I want to make sure we're building on the right
number before I put anything in front of Chase.

Thanks both — the call sounds like it went well and I'll pick up the thread with Chase once
I've got my head around it.

Ethan

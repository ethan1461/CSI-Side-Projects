## The question — your judgement 1, where I think the ordering may be the wrong way round

You judge the **capacity or turnaround** half of the migration ask harder than the **price**
half. **I think the opposite may be true, and it changes which half CSI leads with.**

§2.4 says *"it is expected that change orders may include further financial renumeration or
benefit for Developer."* A standing rate card for migration work therefore **caps the
Developer's upside on work it knows CSI must have**, and does so on an unknown number of future
deals. A **capacity or turnaround commitment**, by contrast, is paid work it can staff for and
plan against — and the 40-hour bucket is a floor on included support, not a ceiling on billable
Excess Support Time at $50/hour (Ex. C §§4.1–4.2).

If that reads correctly, the easier half to agree is capacity, and the harder half is price —
which reverses your ordering. **Neither of us can settle this from the documents**; only Ethan's
conversation tests it. But the amendment scope should probably present both halves without
ranking which is harder, or state the ranking as an open question.

   per-acquisition channel cost, not only in the amendment scope.

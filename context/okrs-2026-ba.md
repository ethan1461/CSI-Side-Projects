# Booking Agents

## Role Overview

Booking agents execute domestic and international bookings accurately using the OMS.

## Key Responsibilities

- Process bookings in OMS

- Validate airline and customer data

- Coordinate with airlines and funeral homes

- Ensure documentation accuracy

- Meet turnaround time SLAs

## Success in This Role Looks Like

- High booking accuracy

- Consistent OMS usage

- Positive customer interactions

- Strong collaboration

## Reporting & Collaboration

Report to the Directors of Bookings.

## Performance Scorecard

### Objective

Execute accurate and timely bookings.

### Key Results (Bonus‑Eligible)

#### KR1: Meet booking volume targets

#### This measures productivity relative to expected workload and case complexity. Consistent achievement of volume expectations is required, while exceeding targets without quality degradation is rewarded.

#### KR2: Maintain ≥99% booking accuracy

#### This tracks bookings that require no correction, airline follow-up, or internal rework. Accuracy reflects attention to detail, professionalism, and adherence to process.

#### Accuracy is tracked by:

#### OMS error logs

#### Airline corrections

#### Internal audit review

#### KR3: Meet SLA turnaround ≥95%

#### This measures responsiveness and reliability from booking initiation to submission. Delays caused by avoidable errors or missed follow-ups negatively impact this metric.

#### Excludes:

#### Customer delays

#### Missing documentation

#### Airline outages

#### KR4: Demonstrate consistent OMS usage

All bookings must be created, edited, and finalized within the OMS. System logs confirm compliance, and reliance on manual workarounds or side channels negatively impacts performance.

#### KR5: New Business Development -- $750/each bonus eligible

For any net-new funeral home (has never booked with CSI before), that you have directly sourced the business of and generates $2000+ in revenue this year, you will receive an additional $750 towards your bonus. This is meant to incentivize you to try and get new business during hours where you are not needed to support on the booking’s workflow. You must have clear, written documentation detailing relationship origination or something to verify that you sourced the opportunity.

**Subject:** Quick help on how we charge for the extra services

Hi Maya,

I'm trying to build a proper picture of where our money actually comes from, and there's a
gap I can't fill without you.

We're good at tracking the air freight side — what we charge and what the airline costs us.
**What I don't have is anything on the extras**: consulate work, apostilles, document
handling, pickups and drop-offs, and whatever else gets added to a booking.

I'm told you're the person who knows how these actually get charged.

**What would help most** — just the fee types, not individual cases:

1. **What do we charge for, and roughly how much?** Consulate and consular documentation,
   apostilles, pickup, drop-off, anything else that shows up as an extra line.
2. **Is each one a set fee, or does it vary?** And if it varies, what drives it — country,
   urgency, how much work it takes?
3. **What does each one cost us?** If we pay a consulate a fee, or pay a courier, or pay
   someone to run a document across town — roughly what is it?
4. **Are there any we do for free** that take real time?

A rough list is genuinely fine. I'm not after precision to the dollar, I'm trying to find
out whether these services make us money, break even, or quietly cost us — and right now I
have no idea which.

**No need to pull individual bookings** — the fee types and typical amounts are all I need.

If it's easier to talk it through than write it out, I'm happy to do that instead.

Thanks Maya — this is one of the bigger holes in what I can see at the moment, and you're
the only one who can fill it.

Ethan

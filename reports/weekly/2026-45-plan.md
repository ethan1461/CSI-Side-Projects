## 4. The dynamic-pricing split

You gave the hypothesis to data-analyst. It spans two agents and `§7.6` governs how it may
be answered at all, so I split it three ways and reframed the question once.

- **data-analyst (TASK-0002) owns testability.** With no quote log, no win/loss log and no
  booking-level data, my expectation is the honest answer is *"not testable today"* — and
  the deliverable is then the smallest instrumentation that makes it testable, not a
  pricing view. Descriptive only; no price recommendation.
- **pricing-strategist (TASK-0005) owns structure.** Where CSI is underpriced relative to
  **its own cost and the complexity of the service** — the lane, the paperwork, the service
  level. Not relative to who the customer is.
- **Neither agent produces a customer-segment price list.** This is the reframe, and it is
  the one thing in this plan I am pushing back on. "Charge certain funeral homes more" is
  one step from a `§7.6` violation the moment "certain" resolves to a diaspora corridor —
  and it would be a reputational weapon pointed at ourselves in a business where the buyer
  is already carrying a family's grief. The legitimate version of your hypothesis is
  **price to complexity and cost to serve, which genuinely differ by lane**, and I think
  that version is also the bigger number. Said once; your call.
- **The decision stays with you and Donald**, and it is already agenda item 3 of the DR-001
  meeting. Good structure — I am not building a parallel one.

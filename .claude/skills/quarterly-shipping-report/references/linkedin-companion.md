# LinkedIn Companion Content

The report is the anchor; the LinkedIn posts are how it travels. Produce an announcement post that ships with the report, plus 2–4 standalone thought pieces that can be posted in the weeks between quarterly reports to keep CSI visible.

Same voice as the report: authoritative, dignified, useful, never salesy. LinkedIn rewards a strong first line (the "hook") because only the first ~2 lines show before "…see more."

## 1. Announcement post (ships with the report)

Purpose: drive reads of the new quarterly report.

Structure:
- **Hook (line 1):** a single finding from the quarter, stated plainly. "This quarter, more families brought loved ones home through [corridor] than any other route we serve."
- **2–4 short lines:** the most interesting takeaways, as a tight teaser — not the whole report.
- **The why:** one line on why this matters to directors (planning, lead times, corridor pressure).
- **CTA:** a clear, low-key invitation to read the full report (link or "full report in comments").
- **Tone:** proud but understated. Let the insight pull the click.

Keep it to ~120–180 words. A few line breaks, no walls of text. Hashtags sparingly (#funeralprofession #repatriation and similar) — 3–5 max.

## 2. Standalone thought pieces (2–4, post over the weeks following)

Each takes *one* idea from the report and stands entirely on its own — a reader who never saw the report still gets full value. These are the steady drumbeat that builds the "CSI is the expert" reputation between quarterly drops.

Good sources for these: the Trend Spotlight, one striking Lane Watch item, one "what this means for funeral homes" takeaway, or one corridor story.

Structure for each:
- **Hook (line 1):** a sharp observation, a surprising number, or a useful question. Make the reader stop scrolling.
- **The insight (body):** 100–200 words developing the one idea. Teach something. Keep paragraphs to 1–3 lines for mobile.
- **The takeaway:** what the reader should do or remember.
- **Soft close:** an understated CSI presence — "It's the kind of pattern you see when you move loved ones through eight U.S. gateways every day" — or a genuine question that invites comments. No hard pitch.

### Example hooks (illustrative — replace with the quarter's real material)

- "A booking that takes three days to confirm isn't a booking — it's a family waiting."
- "The busiest repatriation corridor in the U.S. right now isn't the one most directors would guess."
- "When a single airline drops a route, a grieving family in another state feels it. Here's how."
- "Cremation is rising — but long-distance repatriation isn't going away. It's changing shape."

## Cadence suggestion (optional, offer if useful)

- Week 1: announcement post + report.
- Weeks 2–6: one thought piece each, spaced out.
- Result: ~6 weeks of steady, expert-voiced visibility per quarterly report — a repeatable content engine off a single data pull.

## Guardrails (same as the report)

- Real data only; never fabricate a statistic to make a punchier hook. Use `[FILL: …]` if needed.
- Dignified always — these posts are public and the audience is the profession.
- Don't name and knock competitors.

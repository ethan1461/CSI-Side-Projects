## The note

**Subject:** Education on shipping and transporting remains — would ICCFA be open to this?

Hi Lindsy,

It was great chatting with you at NFDA.

As I mentioned, we'd love to help ICCFA build formal education on shipping and transporting human
remains. A course like this used to exist in the profession and has since gone away, and the need
has only grown. Repatriation is rising, international shipments involve consulates, customs and
airline requirements most directors rarely see, and a mistake at any step lands on a grieving
family.

What we have in mind:

- **A masterclass covering domestic and international shipping** — airline requirements,
  documentation, consulate work, customs clearance, and what to do when plans change
- **A curriculum we can design ourselves or co-create with ICCFA**, built to qualify for CE credit
  if that's something the programme supports
- **Delivery at state and national shows across the country**, run by our team

Cargo Sales International has spent 23 years doing exactly this. We move more than 11,000 loved
ones a year through eight major U.S. gateways, so the course would be grounded in what actually
happens on the ground rather than in theory.

To be clear, this would be education, not a sales pitch.

Would you be open to a short call in the next couple of weeks to talk through whether this fits
ICCFA's education plans, and who else should be in the conversation?

Thanks Lindsy,

Ethan

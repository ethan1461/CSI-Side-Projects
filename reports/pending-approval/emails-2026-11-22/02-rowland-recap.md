## The note

**Subject:** Following up — door-to-door and next steps

Hello,

Thank you for the time this week. It was a genuinely useful conversation and I wanted to put
down what we took from it, so we are working from the same page.

**What we think we agreed in principle**

You can handle collection and onward delivery across the UK and into other European
countries — from the arrival airport through to a funeral home or a family's door. That
includes cremated remains, which matters to us more than it might sound: it is the part of
an international shipment where our service has always stopped short.

Your drivers deliver direct, and because you have tracking in your vehicles you can give us
confirmation of delivery and a time. **That last point is the one we would most like to get
right.** Telling a funeral director that their loved one landed is useful; telling them it
was delivered at a quarter past two is a different quality of service, and it is not
something we can offer at the European end today.

**Where we fit, so there are no surprises**

U.S. exports are the basis of our business. We move around 11,000 loved ones a year across
eight U.S. gateways, and a great deal of our international work is the outbound leg — getting
someone out of the United States and onto the right aircraft. We also work closely with
Bergen Funeral Service, who I know are a significant partner of yours; we handle much of
their international outbound and act as their 24/7 desk. **Nothing we are discussing changes
that, and it is not our intention to come between you.** What we are talking about is the
part of the journey we cannot reach — once the aircraft has landed in Europe.

The pattern we see most often is a flight into London and then an onward leg, so that is
probably where this is most immediately useful.

**On pricing**

You mentioned you normally charge by the mile but that at volume a flat fee becomes possible,
and that you would put a rough sheet together. That would be very welcome — once we can see
it, we can be specific rather than theoretical.

To help you price it properly, **we will send you the lanes and volumes we actually run into
Europe**, so you are quoting against real traffic rather than an estimate. Give me a few days
to pull it together.

**What would be useful from your side**

1. The rough pricing sheet, whenever it is ready.
2. A short note on what happens operationally once a shipment is booked with us and handed
   over — what you need from us, when, and in what form.
3. How the tracking and delivery confirmation would reach us. Even a simple email at the
   point of delivery would be a step forward; if there is something more automated, better
   still.

No rush on any of it over the holiday period at our end. Thank you again — this is the first
conversation we have had about Europe that felt like it solved a real problem rather than
adding a step.

Best regards,

Ethan Silvey
Chief Strategy Officer, Cargo Sales International
